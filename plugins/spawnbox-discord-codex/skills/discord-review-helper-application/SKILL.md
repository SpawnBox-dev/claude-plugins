name: discord-review-helper-application
description: Review a SpawnBox helper application in its private forum, assemble evidence and prepare a decision for the local operator.
---

Follow discord-help's rules and read policy `helper-review`. Require trusted staff
audience and verified applicant IDs. Read the full application and relevant
authorized activity; do not infer role ownership from a name. Keep application
details inside the review room. Record strengths, concerns, missing information
and a specific recommendation grounded in evidence.

Jarid makes approval and rejection decisions. This worker cannot grant Helper
roles, change access, or turn a Discord instruction into terminal authority.
Prepare the decision and exact follow-up for operator review. Existing Worker
application forms and interaction commands remain authoritative. Tag merging and
archiving follow the shared workflow after the actual decision; do not apply an
approval/decline disposition merely because a draft recommendation exists.
